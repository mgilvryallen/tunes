# Tunes

A tunebook for the tunes you know (or are learning): old-time, Irish, Scottish, Québécois and more.

**Use it:** https://mgilvryallen.github.io/tunes/

- Keep each tune's key, mode, type, tunings (one or more, including your own), author, a recording link, and notes. Tap a tune for its own page with the dots and a drone in its key.
- Mark each one **learning**, **getting it**, or **solid**, and search or filter the list.
- **Styles:** give each tune one or more styles (Old-time, Irish, Scottish, Cape Breton, Québécois, Shetland, Scandinavian, Bluegrass), then tap any mix of styles to narrow the list.
- **Sheet music from anywhere:** link a setting on The Session, paste ABC from abcnotation.com or the Traditional Tune Archive, or add a photo from your camera roll (photos stay on the device).
- **Chords:** a chord chart for each tune, pulled from Session settings that have chords, or written yourself: spaces between bars ("D D G A"), two chords together share a bar ("AD"), a blank line starts the next part.
- **Sort** A–Z, by key, style, meter or tuning, with buttons to narrow to the ones you want.
- **Any size:** − and + fit more or fewer bars on each line, so the music always fills the screen.
- **Sets:** pick tunes (the ones in a tuning you can play next come first) and put them in order (move them up and down, or edit a saved set later), or let it suggest a few that change key the way contra sets do (up a step, a fourth or a fifth; old-time sets stay in one key). A set opens like a music stand, with each tune's dots one after another (side by side on a big screen).
- **Sync (optional):** keep the same tunebook on your phone and computer through a private file on your own GitHub (the same token works for Gas and Tunes).
- **The Session:** type a new tune's name and it looks it up on [thesession.org](https://thesession.org). Pick the tune, then pick a setting (you see its first two bars), and it fills in the key, mode and type, links the tune, and keeps that setting's notation so **Show the dots** works offline.
- **Quick Add:** paste a whole list at once, one per line, like `Sail Away Ladies, D, reel`. Then let it look up the ones without sheet music on The Session: tunes whose name, type and key line up get linked by themselves (each one's page asks if it's the right tune), and you pick from the rest.
- Every tune has a **Drone** button that opens [Drone](https://mgilvryallen.github.io/drone/) in its key.

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app**.

Your tunes live only on your device. Tap **Save Backup** now and then, and **Restore From Backup** on a new phone.

## Make your own copy

Click **Use this template** on GitHub, then **Settings → Pages**, source `main`. Everything is in `index.html`.
